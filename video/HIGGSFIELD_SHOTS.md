# Optional generated shots (Higgsfield)

The explainer works without these. Generated shots are only for mood in the
opening and closing; every shot of EIDOS itself stays real screen footage.

## 1. Opening, 6 to 8 s (replaces the tile wall)

Save as `public/clips/hook_broll.mp4` (16:9). The hook scene picks it up automatically.

> Slow dolly forward through an endless dark space filled with thousands of floating
> glowing laptop and monitor screens, each playing a different software tutorial with a
> mouse cursor moving. Cool blue-white light, shallow depth of field, gentle parallax,
> cinematic, calm, no text, no logos, no people.

## 2. Bridge into "EIDOS", 3 to 4 s (optional)

> Macro shot of a single glowing screen in a dark room; a thin dashed blue outline
> traces around one spreadsheet row and turns green. Minimal, clean, cinematic, no text.

## 3. Closing, 4 to 5 s (optional, behind the final line)

> Pull back from one screen to reveal a vast grid of screens, each showing a small green
> check mark appearing in sequence, like a wave. Calm, hopeful, cool light, no text.

After adding clips, re-render: `npx remotion render EidosExplainer out/EIDOS_explainer.mp4 --concurrency=2`
